# Comprehensive User Guide

Welcome to the Blood Donor Finder & Blood Bank Management System User Guide.

---

## 1. DONOR Guide
1. **Registration:** Open the app $\rightarrow$ Select **Register** $\rightarrow$ Fill in your details $\rightarrow$ Select **DONOR** role $\rightarrow$ Choose your blood group and town/city.
2. **Managing Availability:** From the **Donor Dashboard**, tap **Update Availability & Location**. Toggle switch to `Available for Contact` when ready to donate, or disable when unavailable.
3. **Responding to Requests:** Open **Blood Request Invitations** to view emergency requests in your area. Tap **Accept** to share contact details with the requester/hospital, or **Decline**.

---

## 2. REQUESTER Guide
1. **Searching Donors:** From the **Requester Dashboard**, select **Search Potential Donors**. Filter by required Blood Group and City/Town.
2. **Creating a Request:** Select **Create Blood Request** $\rightarrow$ Enter required blood group, units needed, urgency level, hospital name, and contact phone number $\rightarrow$ Tap **Submit Request**.
3. **Tracking Progress:** View **My Blood Requests** to see status updates (`OPEN`, `RESPONSES_RECEIVED`, `IN_PROGRESS`, `FULFILLED`).

---

## 3. HOSPITAL STAFF Guide
1. **Access Authorization:** Hospital staff accounts must be verified by a System Administrator before clinical privileges are enabled.
2. **Clinical Blood Requests:** Create official hospital requests directly linked to your medical facility.
3. **Querying Blood Banks:** Use **Query Blood Bank Stock** to inspect real-time verified units at linked blood banks before requesting transfers.

---

## 4. BLOOD BANK OFFICER Guide
1. **Logging Received Stock:** From the **Blood Bank Dashboard**, tap **Record Verified Stock Receipt** $\rightarrow$ Input blood group, unit count, collection date, expiry date, and donor reference.
2. **Issuing Stock:** Log stock issues or hospital transfers using **Issue Stock**. The system enforces transaction checks to prevent issuing unverified or negative stock.
3. **Alerts:** Check low-stock and expiring unit alerts to maintain optimal inventory turnover.

---

## 5. SYSTEM ADMINISTRATOR Guide
1. **Organization Verification:** Open **Organization Verification** to review pending hospital and blood bank registration requests. Verify legitimate credentials before granting professional roles.
2. **Audit Logs:** Monitor system security and administrative actions in real time via **System Audit Logs**.
